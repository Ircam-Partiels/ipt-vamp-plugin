Models
------

The models embedded in the Resources/models folder of the plugin bundle are the
IPT models by Nicolas Brochec, Joakim Borg and Marco Fiorini at IRCAM
(http://repmus.ircam.fr/ipt/home), trained and exported to TorchScript with
ipt_recognition (https://github.com/nbrochec/ipt_recognition). They are
distributed under the license of the libipt project
(https://github.com/nbrochec/libipt), the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International Public License (CC BY-NC 4.0,
ipt-models.txt, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): commercial
use of the models is not permitted. The files are renamed but not modified.

- 00_eguitar.ts (eguitar_ircam.ts, electric guitar)
  https://huggingface.co/nbrochec/ipt_models/blob/main/eguitar_ircam.ts
- 01_flute.ts (flute_ircam.ts, flute)
  https://huggingface.co/nbrochec/ipt_models/blob/main/flute_ircam.ts
- 02_trumpet.ts (trumpet_ircam.ts, trumpet)
  https://huggingface.co/nbrochec/ipt_models/blob/main/trumpet_ircam.ts
- 03_trumpet_harmon.ts (trumpet_harmon_ircam.ts, trumpet with harmon mute)
  https://huggingface.co/nbrochec/ipt_models/blob/main/trumpet_harmon_ircam.ts
